## BMW Sales & Revenue Analytics Dashboard | Power BI
## Project Overview
Developed an end-to-end Power BI Business Intelligence solution to analyze BMW sales, revenue, and performance trends across multiple dimensions including time, geography, product models, and sales channels.
The dashboard delivers interactive, KPI-driven insights to support strategic decision-making and performance monitoring.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze sales performance and revenue growth over time
- Identify top-performing and high-value vehicle models
- Compare current year vs previous year metrics (YoY analysis)
- Evaluate regional and channel-wise sales distribution
- Provide an executive-level analytical view using dynamic dashboards

## Business Questions Addressed

- What are the revenue and quantity sold trends by month and year?
- Which BMW models contribute most to total revenue and sales volume?
- Which countries and regions show the highest growth?
- How do sales channels (Online, Dealership, Wholesale) perform?
- What are the highest average selling price models?
- How does current performance compare with previous periods?

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Revenue
- Revenue Growth % (Year-over-Year)
- Total Units Sold
- Average Selling Price
- Top Selling Models
- Most Expensive Models
- Revenue & Quantity (Current vs Previous Year)
- Sales by Country
- Sales by Channel

## Data Analysis Process

- Performed data cleaning, transformation, and normalization using Power Query
- Designed an optimized star-schema data model with fact and dimension tables
- Built DAX measures for revenue, YoY growth, previous year comparison, and KPIs
- Implemented time intelligence calculations (monthly, yearly, trend analysis)
- Developed interactive dashboards with slicers, filters, and drill-downs
- Applied best practices in data visualization and BI storytelling

## Key Insights & Findings

- Achieved ~25% year-over-year revenue growth, indicating strong business performance
- BMW 3 Series and BMW 5 Series emerged as consistent top-selling models
- Wholesale and Dealership channels generated the majority of sales volume
- Certain countries showed double-digit YoY growth, highlighting expansion opportunities
- Premium models (BMW iX3, BMW M5) delivered high revenue per unit, improving profitability
- Clear seasonal sales patterns identified, supporting demand forecasting
